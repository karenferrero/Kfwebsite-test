Trellix·2024–2025

Turning customer health into renewals

A customer engagement program that grew 370% in a year, lifted renewal rates to 87%, and anchored a $34M two-year renewal for one of the company's most demanding programs.

The challenge

Trellix, the company formed from FireEye and McAfee Enterprise, had a services organization with deep technical talent and a thin picture of which customers were actually at risk. Renewal conversations started late and started cold.

At the same time, the Google Reverse Engineering program was growing faster than the team delivering it, with a two-year renewal on the line and no margin for a slip in quality.

What I led

  • Directed the Health Watch program: a structured cadence of proactive customer touchpoints tied to product adoption, open incidents, and renewal timing.
  • Scaled the program from a pilot to roughly 2,000 touchpoints in 2024, a 370% increase on the prior year, without adding proportional headcount.
  • Owned the Google Reverse Engineering program relationship and grew its delivery team 237% to keep pace with demand.
  • Rebuilt the incident workflow around human-machine collaboration so that analysts spent their time on judgement, not triage.

What changed

Account teams knew which customers needed attention months before renewal, and had a record of value delivered to bring to the conversation. Accounts that went through the program were 17% more likely to renew than those that did not.

Program output rose 74% year over year while the team handled more than 3,000 incident responses at a near-zero error rate.

The result

Renewal rates for engaged accounts reached 87%. The Google Reverse Engineering program renewed for two years at $34M, with a team built to deliver it.
