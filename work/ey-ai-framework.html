EY-Parthenon·2026–present

A repeatable model for client AI adoption

Building the unified AI transformation and go-to-market framework used across EY-Parthenon's Western Europe practice, so every country team guides clients the same way from pilot to scale.

The challenge

Every country team in the practice was having the same client conversation about AI, and every team was having it differently. Pre-sales assets were bespoke, the adoption journey was redrawn for each pursuit, and sector expertise lived in people rather than in anything reusable.

The CTO office needed one model that a team in any market could pick up and run, without flattening the sector nuance that wins the work.

What I led

  • Designed a unified AI transformation and go-to-market framework that describes the client journey from first use case to governed, scaled adoption.
  • Authored the AI Playbook: the reusable core asset that country teams use to structure client conversations, proposals, and delivery.
  • Built sector activation materials across more than ten industries, so the framework arrives with the examples a client in that sector expects.
  • Worked directly with country-level teams to adopt the model and feed what they learned back into it.

What changed

Teams spend their preparation time on the client's specifics instead of rebuilding the basics. Proposals in different markets now describe the same journey in the same language.

The practice has a shared, improving asset base for AI work rather than a collection of individual decks.

The result

The framework is adopted across Western Europe, with playbooks and sector materials scaling AI enablement and pre-sales consistency across 10+ industry sectors. The engagement is ongoing, and outcomes will be reported as they are verified.
