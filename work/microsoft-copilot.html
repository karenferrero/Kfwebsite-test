Scaling Copilot adoption across government

How a national technical sales team took Microsoft 365 Copilot and the Modern Work portfolio into State & Local Government and finished the year at 134.5% of a $1B+ quota.

The challenge

State and local agencies wanted what generative AI promised and were nervous about everything it implied: data boundaries, procurement rules, accessibility obligations, and staff who had never seen a Copilot prompt. The team selling into them was spread across the country, and every specialist was solving the same objections from scratch.

The quota was larger than a billion dollars. Winning at that scale meant the team could not rely on individual heroics. It needed a shared way of working.

What I led

  • Led the national Technical Specialist team responsible for Copilot and digital-transformation adoption across State & Local Government.
  • Built the Copilot Readiness playbook, giving every specialist one path for taking an agency from curiosity to a governed, licensed rollout.
  • Built the Google Compete playbook so that head-to-head evaluations were run with the same evidence and the same story in every state.
  • Stood up virtual teams across the org to capture what was working in one region and make it standard everywhere.
  • Served as Accessibility Lead for the segment and as a formal mentor in Microsoft's Neurodiverse Mentoring program.

What changed

Specialists stopped improvising. The playbooks turned the hardest conversations, readiness and competition, into repeatable motions, and the virtual teams kept them current as the product changed month to month.

The team culture held up under the quota. The leader score for work-life flexibility came in 25 points above the company average, which mattered because the people carrying the number stayed.

The result

The team closed FY23 at 134.5% of a quota above $1B, and the Copilot Readiness and Google Compete playbooks became the org-wide standard for the segment.
