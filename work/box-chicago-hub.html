Box·2018–2020

Building a consulting hub from zero

Standing up Box's Chicago consulting hub, sponsoring enterprise customers through renewal and expansion, and redesigning delivery so it scaled without eroding margin.

The challenge

Box had enterprise customers across the central United States and Canada and no local consulting presence to serve them. Projects were staffed from the coasts, margins were under pressure, and the region was behind on services quota.

The job was to build the hub, win back the number, and prove the model could scale.

What I led

  • Built the Chicago consulting hub, hiring the team and establishing it as the delivery centre for the region.
  • Served as executive sponsor for enterprise clients, owning the relationship through renewal and expansion conversations.
  • Designed and implemented an offshore delivery model with TaskUs, moving repeatable work off the most expensive people without changing what customers experienced.

What changed

Customers in the region had a senior person in their time zone who could commit the company. Renewals and expansions that had been at risk closed.

Delivery capacity grew faster than headcount. Projects that once waited for a scarce consultant could start when the customer was ready.

The result

The region reached 110% of services quota in Q3 FY20 and delivery margin improved 22%, with a hub that outlasted the person who built it.
