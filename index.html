JobCompass

About

Background

I started in enterprise services delivery and spent two decades learning how large technology companies actually make money from customers after the contract is signed. At Cisco I ran a $54M Services business and the delivery relationships for State Farm, Allstate, Caterpillar, and CNA. At Box I built a consulting hub from nothing. At Microsoft I led the technical sales team that took Copilot into State & Local Government. At Trellix I turned a customer health program into a renewal engine.

Along the way I became a hands-on AI practitioner, not just a sponsor of it. I design curricula, write playbooks, and build tools, and I've found that the building is what makes the strategy credible.

Inclusion and accessibility are core to how I lead. I served as Accessibility Lead for Microsoft's State & Local Government business, mentored in Microsoft's Neurodiverse Mentoring program, and have spent more than a decade on nonprofit boards serving people with disabilities.
